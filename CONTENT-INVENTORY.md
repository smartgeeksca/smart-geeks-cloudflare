## Deliberately not included in this delivery

The brief says location pages (Surrey/Delta/Langley/White Rock ×
device-type combinations) "may be included only when they contain
genuinely useful, unique content" -- optional language, and they are not in
the 27 required pages. They were left out of this delivery rather than
generated as thin, templated mail-merge pages, consistent with that
constraint and with the prior audit's own P2 roadmap note on this exact
point. `src/content/services.py` and `src/data/business.py` already carry
per-service and per-area data, so genuinely distinct location pages could be
added later without restructuring anything.

A blog was part of an earlier, separate build of this site but is not in
this brief's 27 required pages, so it isn't included here either.
