# Visual Storyboard — What a Visitor Sees, Section by Section

Per the brief's constraint (no people, no technician stock photos, no call-center imagery, no cookie-cutter marketing images), every visual in this storyboard is either real typography/layout composition or the CSS-only "signature surface" (gradient mesh + technical grid lines) established in V3 — nothing here is a placeholder for a photo that doesn't exist. `[BUILD-VERIFIED]` marks what's actually live; `[RECOMMENDATION]` marks the one item still pending an owner decision.

1. **Hero** — sees a full-bleed dark navy field with a faint technical grid and two soft glow points (blue upper-right, green lower-left), a small glass "status" pill with a pulsing-green dot, then an oversized white headline. No image, no icon — the type itself is the visual. `[BUILD-VERIFIED]`

2. **Stat strip** — sees three large mono numerals on a slightly lighter dark band, evenly divided by hairline verticals — reads like an instrument-panel readout, not a marketing claim. `[BUILD-VERIFIED]`

3. **Statement band** — sees one sentence, alone, at roughly 2.5–3x normal heading size, on plain white — the page's first deliberate silence after two dense dark sections. `[BUILD-VERIFIED]`

4. **Repair intelligence** — sees, on the left, four short philosophy points each marked by a vertical rule (not a bullet, not an icon); on the right, a dashed vertical trace line connecting four mono-numbered nodes — visually reads as a circuit trace, reinforcing "we think about this like engineers" without a single fabricated diagram image. `[BUILD-VERIFIED]`

5. **Device ecosystem** — sees three labeled clusters of small icon-plus-text rows (using the site's existing original SVG icon set, not photography), then a full-width dark band with its own icon and a right-pointing arrow — visually distinct from the light clusters above it, correctly signaling "this one thing is different in kind, not just another item." `[BUILD-VERIFIED]`

6. **Buy/Sell/Trade flagship** — sees, on the left, the existing circuit-pattern `.img-frame` treatment (a blue/green gradient panel with a large trade/exchange-arrows glyph), and on the right, real copy plus a row of pill-shaped tags listing exactly what's accepted. `[BUILD-VERIFIED]`

7. **Local authority** — sees plain prose on the left and, on the right, a monospaced "readout" card — label/value pairs (Location / Phone / Email) styled like a system status panel rather than a normal address block. `[BUILD-VERIFIED]`

8. **FAQ** — sees a plain accordion list, deliberately unchanged in visual register from before — the one place on the page where "more reading, less seeing" is actually appropriate, since an FAQ's job is to be searched and read, not glanced at. `[BUILD-VERIFIED]`

9. **CTA band** — sees the same dark signature surface as the hero, closing the page on the same visual note it opened on. `[BUILD-VERIFIED]`

10. **Footer** — sees the existing four-column dark footer with its gradient top accent line. `[BUILD-VERIFIED]`

**The one open item:** every "photograph" a visitor sees on this site is still the CSS-only circuit-pattern treatment, not real photography of motherboards, laptop internals, or the physical Surrey storefront. That was flagged as an owner action in every previous pass and remains one — nothing in V4 changes that recommendation, and nothing in V4 pretends otherwise. `[RECOMMENDATION]` (unchanged from prior reports)
